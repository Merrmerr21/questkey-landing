MOBILE TEXT READABILITY (CRITICAL — FIX FIRST)
The site looks good on desktop and tablet but text is too small and cramped on mobile (below 768px). Before doing anything else, fix these issues:
The three strategy comparison cards (Airbnb, Mid-Term, Long-Term) in the property demo section are being forced side-by-side on mobile. The text is getting cut off and the numbers are barely readable. On mobile, stack these three cards vertically instead of side-by-side. Each card should be full-width with the strategy name, cash flow number, cap rate, occupancy, and the tag (Recommended Path, Solid Fallback, Negative CF) all clearly readable at a glance. Keep the horizontal layout on tablet and desktop.
The monospace AGENT OUTPUT text inside each agent card is too small on mobile. It looks like it is rendering around 11-12px. Increase it to at least 14px on mobile. If the monospace font is making it feel cramped, switch to the regular body font on mobile and use a light background color or left border to differentiate the output block instead. Set line-height to at least 1.5 so the text breathes.
The fake browser URL bar text (app.yieldkey.com/deal/1248-oak…) is tiny on mobile. Either hide the URL bar on mobile and just keep the three colored dots as a minimal browser chrome indicator, or increase the text to at least 12px.
Do a full pass across every section on mobile and make sure body text is at least 16px, subheadlines are at least 14-15px, no text is clipped or truncated by container overflow, and all cards have at least 16px internal padding so text is never flush against card edges.
